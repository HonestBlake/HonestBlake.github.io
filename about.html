Background

Computer engineering with a software perspective.

I am pursuing a B.S. in Computer Engineering with a minor in Computer Science and an M.S. in Software & Security Engineering at Texas Tech University. That combination lets me work across abstraction layers, from microcontrollers and circuits to APIs, cloud services, and user-facing applications.

My experience includes full-stack development, AI code evaluation, embedded robotics, undergraduate research, and open-source C++ libraries. I enjoy problems where understanding the surrounding system is as important as writing the individual component.

I am particularly interested in systems programming, embedded software, and product engineering roles that involve both careful technical work and collaboration with the people using what gets built.
